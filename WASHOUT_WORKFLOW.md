# Washout workflow

The tender names one requirement: none, sweep-out, standard washout, food-grade washout, sanitization, allergen-specific, kosher-certified, halal-certified, pharmaceutical or customer-specific, or manual shipper approval. Those names are shipper selections. They are not a certification that a wash met a standard.

From equipment review, an authorized shipper can request cleaning. A carrier user, driver, or washout facility can submit evidence. Evidence is refused when the trailer id differs from the assigned trailer or when the altered flag is set. This foundation does not scan files. The altered flag is an input, not a malware engine.

Accepted evidence returns the shipment to equipment review and lets approval proceed. If the tender requires cleaning and no evidence was accepted, approval fails. Rejecting or substituting the trailer does not cancel the carrier.
