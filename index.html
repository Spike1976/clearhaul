NON-NEGOTIABLE RULEOnce delivery is verified, earned freight payment cannot be held hostage.

A documented exception can open a separate dispute or claim. It does not silently freeze the carrier's undisputed payment.

★

Ratings influence trust—not ownership of earned money.

Strong performance can unlock earlier release, higher load limits and fewer manual reviews. Repeated verified failures can reduce access or remove a party from ClearHaul. A bad rating alone never cancels payment for a completed load.

05 · THE FREIGHT FLOW

Six gates.
Zero guesswork.

ClearHaul separates carrier selection, equipment approval, movement and payment so each party knows exactly what was agreed and what comes next.

01POST

Standardize the load

The shipper enters locations, freight, timing, equipment, pricing and compliance information in one format.

02PROTECT

Fund before award

Money is confirmed through a regulated payment partner before a carrier commits equipment.

03MATCH

Select the carrier

Verified carriers compete under disclosed terms. Every bid version and acceptance is preserved.

04APPROVE

Assign the trailer

Only the selected shipper sees relevant history for the assigned trailer and may request a washout.

05MOVE

Put truth in the cab

The driver receives the final packet, safety details, stops, securement requirements and evidence tools.

06PROVE

Deliver and release

Delivery evidence starts the agreed review, payment release and tamper-evident record process.

06 · BUILT FOR THE WHOLE LOAD

Three workspaces.
One shared truth.

SHIPPER WORKSPACE

Post complete freight. Buy verified capacity.

Create a standardized tender, secure funding, compare qualified bids and approve the exact trailer assigned to the load.

  • Structured tender builder
  • Funded-load confirmation
  • Carrier authority snapshot
  • Controlled equipment review
  • Delivery and dispute evidence
CARRIER WORKSPACE

Compete on service. Keep the terms honest.

Find funded freight, submit a versioned bid, assign authorized people and equipment, and follow payment through release.

  • Qualified-load discovery
  • Versioned bids and acceptance
  • Driver and equipment assignment
  • Protected fleet information
  • Payment status and evidence
DRIVER WORKSPACE

Everything needed to move the load. Nothing buried.

Carry the final load packet offline, record arrivals and detention, see safety requirements and submit pickup and delivery proof.

  • Offline load packet
  • Hazmat information handoff
  • Securement and safety checklist
  • Detention evidence timeline
  • Pickup and delivery capture
