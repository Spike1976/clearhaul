The problem

Freight should not require a fog machine.

Rates disappear into private negotiations. Documents get trapped in inboxes. Drivers receive critical details last. Equipment history is either invisible or exposed too broadly. ClearHaul is designed to replace that confusion with a transaction everyone can follow.

01

Disconnected information

One standardized load record follows the shipment from posting through payment.

02

Uncertain payment

Loads are funded before award, with visible conditions and an auditable release process.

03

Unclear responsibility

Every confirmation, correction, document and decision is attributed to the responsible party.

One accountable workflow

From posted load to proven delivery.

ClearHaul separates carrier selection from equipment approval and keeps each decision visible.

01

Standardize

The shipper enters locations, freight, timing, equipment, money and compliance data in one consistent format.

02

Fund

The shipment is funded through a regulated payment partner before final award.

03

Select

Qualified carriers bid under disclosed terms. The shipper conditionally selects one carrier.

04

Approve equipment

The carrier assigns a trailer. Only then can that shipper review its relevant history and request cleaning.

05

Move freight

The driver receives the final load packet, documents, safety information and acknowledgments.

06

Verify and pay

Delivery evidence triggers the agreed review and payment release, backed by a tamper-evident audit record.

Built around the people moving freight

Different tools. One record.

SHIPPER WORKSPACE

Post complete freight. Select qualified capacity.

Create a standardized tender, compare bids, review the assigned trailer and release payment under known terms.

  • Standard load tender
  • Funded-before-award status
  • Carrier qualification snapshot
  • Assigned-equipment review
  • Delivery and dispute evidence
CARRIER WORKSPACE

Bid clearly. Protect your equipment data.

See funded loads, disclose exact terms, assign authorized equipment and follow payment from award to release.

  • Verified marketplace access
  • Versioned bids and terms
  • Fleet and driver assignments
  • Controlled history disclosure
  • Payment status and evidence
DRIVER WORKSPACE

The right information reaches the truck.

Receive the final packet offline, record arrivals and detention, capture evidence and submit proof of delivery.

  • Offline load packet
  • Hazmat information handoff
  • Securement checklist
  • Detention timeline
  • Pickup and delivery evidence
