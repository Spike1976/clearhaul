# Governance

Date: 2026-09-30

Michael Stokes is the product owner.

Engineering assumptions are recorded in DECISIONS.md. They are reversible. They are not Michael's product decisions. An engineering assumption in DECISIONS.md can be replaced when he answers the related question in QUESTIONS_FOR_MICHAEL.md.

Engineers do not adopt legal, financial, or hazardous-material rules. Those subjects stay unanswered until Michael decides them, and until a qualified reviewer has reviewed them where a decision says that review is required. No statute, payment rule, or hazardous-material rule is established by engineering work in this repository.

AGPL version 3 is the approved working license for the server and core under CH-D-0014. A final legal and open-source review is still required. See OPEN_SOURCE_GOVERNANCE.md.
