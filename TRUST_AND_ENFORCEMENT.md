# Trust and enforcement

A report names the shipment, both organizations, a category, a narrative, and an evidence id. The starting action is pattern review. Creating a report does not remove anyone.

Permanent removal is refused until an investigator is assigned. Assignment is refused when the investigator's organization is the shipper or the carrier on that shipment. MFA is required for the investigator permission. No investigator account is connected to a real identity provider.

Graduated action names exist: warning, education, enhanced verification, transaction limit, funding reserve, equipment block, temporary suspension, permanent removal, and authority-referral review. Recording a name does not send a notice, suspend a login, or contact an authority. Those effects are not built.

A driver may dispute a compensation disclosure. The public view is the method label only. The private amount is not included. The dispute sets trust review required. It does not publish earnings.
